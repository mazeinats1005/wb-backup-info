WB Backup rclone

WB Backup rclone is a private backup utility operated by Tatyana Zhaminova for her own business systems. It uses rclone and Google Drive to store and verify backup files.

Google authorization connects the owner's local backup tools to the owner's Google Drive. This application is not a public service and does not offer access to other people's accounts.

This website provides application information only. It does not connect to Google Drive or display backup data.
