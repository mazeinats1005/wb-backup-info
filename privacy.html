Privacy policy

WB Backup rclone · Updated October 2, 2026

This application is operated by Tatyana Zhaminova for personal administration of backups of her own business systems.

Google Drive data and permissions

Following the owner's explicit authorization, rclone uses Google Drive access to upload backup files, list backup folders, read file metadata, download backups for verification and restoration, and remove old backup files under the owner's configured retention rules. The application requests the permissions selected during its Google authorization flow.

Storage and use

Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive and may be stored locally for backup preparation and recovery checks. OAuth credentials are kept separately from public website content and source-code repositories. The operator controls backup retention and deletion.

Data accessed through this application is used for backup and recovery. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. This application does not intentionally share backup data with other users. Google processes files stored in Google Drive according to its own service terms and privacy policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's Google account access through Google Account settings. Revoking access prevents subsequent authorized operations but does not automatically delete existing backup files. The owner can delete those files from Google Drive.

This website

These static information pages contain no application analytics, advertising, forms, or Google authorization functionality. GitHub Pages hosts the pages and may process technical request information under GitHub's own privacy policy.
